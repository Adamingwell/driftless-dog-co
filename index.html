How often should your pup be groomed?

Select your dog's coat type below to reveal our professional groomer schedule recommendations and Sparta seasonal maintenance tips.

Optimal Frequency:

Every 4 to 6 Weeks

High Matting Risk
Recommended In-Salon Routine:

Full haircut every 4-6 weeks alternating with a Bath & Tidy in between. Prevents painful matting at friction points (armpits and ears).

Home Care Tip for Driftless Seasons:

Use a metal line comb daily down to the skin. During Wisconsin snow or muddy spring, dry damp paws thoroughly to avoid pelted fur.

Our Story & Philosophy

Calm, Dignified Grooming Tailored to Each Dog's Temperament

Driftless Dog Co LLC was founded on a simple premise: dog grooming should never be scary. Inspired by the calm and resilient bluffs of the Wisconsin Driftless area, our studio is structured to be quiet, cage-free where appropriate, and thoroughly sanitized between visitors.

Help & Information

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before visiting our Sparta salon.

For the health and safety of all canine guests, we require dogs to be current on Rabies (for all pups over 16 weeks) and DHPP/Distemper. We also strongly recommend the Bordetella (Kennel Cough) vaccine. You can upload a photo during confirmation or bring vet paperwork at drop-off.
Most small to medium grooms take between 1.5 to 2 hours. Large breeds or heavy de-shedding coats take roughly 2 to 2.5 hours. Because we schedule 1-on-1, your dog is never sitting in a crate for hours waiting their turn. We text you 20 minutes before they are ready for pickup!
We specialize in patient handling! We introduce equipment slowly, use gentle warm air instead of roaring cage driers, and allow frequent treat praise breaks. If a dog reaches their stress limit, we pause and prioritize their emotional safety over a salon-perfect look.
We practice "humanity over vanity." If a coat is pelted or severely matted to the skin, de-matting causes extreme pain and skin irritation. In these cases, we will safely clip under the mats to provide a fresh start and guide you on an easy home regimen to rebuild their coat.
Yes! Walk-in nail clips and filing are welcome Tuesday through Friday between 11:00 AM and 2:00 PM ($15). For full haircuts and baths, scheduled appointments are required to maintain a calm environment.
